## Four Networks, One Market - Who Wins?

The Latvian fuel retail market is dominated by four networks: Circle K, Virši, Neste, and Viada. Each occupies a different position regarding price, specialty fuel, coverage, and loyalty programmes. This comparison lays everything out side-by-side so you can decide which network to give your business to, or which combination of networks fits your driving life.

## Who Is Each Station For?

**The Budget Driver (Price is Everything):** Use Viada ADUS. Carry a debit or credit card, accept that loyalty programmes do not apply, and save €8+ on a 50-litre diesel fill versus the market average. No other network is close on price.

**The Eco-Conscious Driver:** Use Neste for HVO Renewable Diesel at 2.590 €/L: up to 90% lower lifecycle carbon footprint than fossil diesel. The price premium is the cost of the sustainability benefit.

**The CNG Vehicle Owner:** Use Virši. It is the only network in Latvia with CNG. There is no alternative.

**The LPG-Converted Car:** Viada ADUS offers the cheapest LPG at 0.985 €/L. Circle K and Virši both offer LPG at 1.085 €/L for drivers who prefer a manned station.

**The Loyalty Point Maximiser:** Circle K EXTRA provides the most consistent per-litre discounts with the widest network. EXTRA in combination with Neste visits covers most scenarios.

**The Fleet or Company Driver:** The Baltic-wide validity of the Circle K app and digital receipts make it operationally convenient.

**The High-Mileage Commuter who wants a manned station:** Neste for standard diesel (cheapest among the big three manned networks), using the Neste app.

## Final Verdict

| Category | Winner |
|----------|--------|
| Cheapest fuel overall | Viada ADUS |
| Cheapest diesel at manned station | Neste |
| Best coverage | Circle K |
| Unique specialty fuel | Virši (CNG), Neste (HVO), Circle K (XTL) |
| Best loyalty programme | Circle K EXTRA |
| Best for eco-conscious drivers | Neste |
| Latvian owned | Virši |

There is no single "best" station for every driver. For most people, a practical approach is to use Viada ADUS as the default for price, Circle K as a fallback for coverage when an ADUS is not nearby, and Neste when HVO or the cheapest manned-station diesel is important.
